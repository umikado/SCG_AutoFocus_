Policies: SRT exports always use clip-relative time. LRC offers clip-relative or original-song time. Positive LRC offset delays events; negative times clamp to 0; normalized output omits offset. LRC exporter drops intervals unrepresentable at centisecond precision, recomputes gaps over surviving rows, and keeps the last lyric at a duplicate output start. Generic LRC codec retains duplicate events and offers millisecond precision. Leading literal LRC time-tag text is rejected because standard LRC has no unambiguous escape. Ordinary bracket text is preserved. SRT codec accepts overlapping source cues but clip export truncates overlaps to the next cue start. Invalid/untimed/outside/empty lines and impossible missing-end fallbacks are skipped. SRT positioning extensions, enhanced word-level LRC and ASS are outside MVP.
